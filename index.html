Model (single-excitation manifold, closed-system Schrödinger dynamics): H = Σn Ωn |n⟩⟨n| + J Σn(|n⟩⟨n+1| + h.c.), with Ω₀ = Δ − V (donor), Ωn = −V/n, J = 500 cm−1, V = 2420 cm−1 (P3HT:PCBM-like parameters of the reference). Initial state |ψ(0)⟩ = |donor⟩; evolution ψ(n,t) = Σk ck φk(n) e−iEkt/ℏ, ck = φk(0). Conversion: 1 eV = 8065.54 cm−1; a 2420 cm−1 oscillation period is ≈ 13.8 fs.
